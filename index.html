chrome extension

InstaSave

live

Bulk-download your saved Instagram collections — photos, videos, carousels and metadata. Fully local.

built with
Vue, Vite, Chrome MV3
status
v1.2 on the store, 1.3 packaged

Instagram gives you no way to get your own saved collections back out. InstaSave pages the REST API 50 items at a time instead of scroll-and-scrape, so a large collection is one pass rather than an hour of scrolling, and completed scans cache for 24 hours. Nothing is uploaded — the downloads go straight from Instagram's CDN to your disk.

chrome extension

Ultimate Video Downloader

in progress

Detect, clip and download video from streaming pages. Take the whole thing, or just the part you actually wanted.

built with
Chrome MV3, zero dependencies
handles
HLS, DASH, direct files

Most downloaders pull the entire video and trim afterwards. This one fetches only the segments covering the range you picked, so time and bandwidth scale with your clip and not with the source. A 30-second clip out of a 1.8 GB file fetched about 1% of it. Everything runs locally — no account, no server.
